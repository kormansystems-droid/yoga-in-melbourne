## The hero image needed compositing

Her portrait is an 880×1100 head-and-chest crop. Janita's is the same dimensions but
a mid-shot, so `object-position:center 26%` framed her head and shoulders. The same
treatment on Shelley's tighter crop produced an extreme close-up with the top of her
head cut off.

Fix: a 1840×1100 plate built by extending her own background: the far-left column
of the portrait, which is pure green bokeh with no skin or hair in it, across the
canvas at 60px blur, with the untouched portrait pasted right and feathered in over
340px. Her face is not altered, mirrored or retouched. `object-position` is now
`center 42%`. The same technique made the 1080×1920 reel plate, extending downward.

**The t-card was downscaled** from 880×1100 to 560×700 (139 KB → 62 KB base64) to sit
inside the house norm; the other five cards run 50–122 KB.

**`index.html` is now 941 KB.** That is up from 878 KB and is a real mobile cost given
Instagram is the front door. Every teacher card inlines a full base64 portrait. Moving
them to `img/` files with `loading="lazy"` would cut the page by roughly two-thirds.
Not done; it touches all six cards and is a separate decision.

## The reel, cut down to three frames

Built first as the untested experiment `YIM-instagram-acquisition-2026-08-19.md`
prescribes at point 4 (lead with the map, mention the episode second), then cut twice
by Mark on 1 Sep. Final form:

`01-opener-teacher` / `02-story` / `03-retreat` / `04-closer`, 17.0s, 1080x1920, silent.

Frame 1 announces that her profile is live. Frame 2 merges the biography beat and her
pull quote, which were separate cards and said the same thing twice. Frame 3 is the
Kula Muriwai retreat. Frame 4 sends the viewer to read the profile and hear her story
at yogainmelbourne.com.au.

**The retreat card renders two ways.** A 1080x1920 photograph at
`reel/shelley/retreat.jpg` makes it a photo card; with no file there it falls back to
a paper card in ink on cream, which is a designed state and not a placeholder. It is
currently the paper card: the device bridge was down for the whole session and the
photograph was never staged.

**Nothing perishable is on it.** As of the 20 Aug interview the retreat had "two
places left" and still has no published dates, price or booking link. A reel outlives
the day it is posted, and a sold-out retreat advertising places is worse than no card.
The card states the shape of the thing; details belong on her profile once they exist.

**The podcast is not named anywhere in the reel.** "Hear her story" routes to the
player that already sits on her profile page, so the episode does not need its own
frame. This sits on the right side of the 19 Aug finding rather than against it: that
note measured pieces presenting *the publication and its people* at 2.5 to 3% follows
per viewer against 0 to 0.5% for podcast-episode promos, and a profile announcement is
the former. What is gone is the explicit map proposition in the opening seconds, so
the publication now rests on the closer alone. If conversion comes in low, restoring
an opener card is the first thing to try, and both the original reasoning and the
reasons for each cut are in `build_solo_reel.py`'s docstring.

**Judge on profile visits per viewer.** Baselines: Alessia's podcast reel 1.27%,
Alessia's carousel 4.35%.

A reel carries no link sticker, so it buys reach and not clicks. The same three PNGs
run as a story with a link sticker per frame if clicks are wanted too.

**Largest uncaptured lever: invite Shelley as a collaborator.** The collab reel
returned 50 followers from 3,200 views; mentioned story frames beat un-mentioned ones
3.4x. Free, and it puts the reel in her followers' feeds.

listing page was regenerated**. Most of those diffs are punctuation inside comments.

**Three em dashes are deliberately left in `data/schedule.json`**, and they are the
one judgement call worth Mark's attention. They are quoted verbatim below, inside code
spans, so this note contains three em dashes on purpose; do not "fix" them here:

- `Flight School — a workshop series with Eliza & Gisele`
- `Strong Asana — community workshop with Masha`
- `Go WITH/N to WIN — September Challenge`

These are **event titles supplied by the studios**, not YiM's voice. Rewriting them
prints a title the studio did not use, which is the same principle as the name-spelling
decision of 17 to 18 Aug. They would also be overwritten on the next pull, so a manual
edit does not hold. If the ban is meant to cover them, the fix belongs in
`pull/normalizers.py` as a transform on ingest, and it should be a conscious choice to
restyle other organisations' titles.
