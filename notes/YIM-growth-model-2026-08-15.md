# Yoga in Melbourne — Growth Model Note

**Date:** 15 August 2026
**Status:** Working strategy note. Supersedes the profile-led growth assumption in earlier planning.
**Author:** Drafted with Claude from a working session with Mark Korman, 15 Aug 2026.

---

## 1. The shift in one line

YiM no longer grows by recruiting teachers to profile. It grows by **publishing through an
expanding network of teachers** — and profiles become one output of that network rather than
the engine that drives it.

Put commercially: **YiM has stopped buying distribution at asset prices.**

Profiles continue, because they are the durable asset and because Mark wants to make them.
What changes is that Instagram cadence and audience growth are no longer hostage to profile
throughput.

---

## 2. Why the old engine capped growth

The original loop was linear:

> find teacher → secure commitment → interview → write profile → publish → IG collab →
> access their audience → repeat

Every new burst of distribution required another completed profile. Five collaborators meant:

- 5 high-intent approaches (and, realistically, 10+ approaches to land 5 yeses)
- 5 × substantial teacher effort
- 5 article compositions
- 5 podcast productions

…to produce **one** distribution event.

Two hidden costs beyond the labour:

1. **Every "no" is permanent.** Asking a second time after a declined profile reads as
   recruitment and burns the relationship.
2. **The ask is high-exposure.** A profile asks someone to be *the subject* — to reveal their
   history and record long-form answers. That is a large risk for an unfamiliar teacher.

---

## 3. The new engine

Five *comments* instead of five *profiles*:

- 5 soft approaches (higher accept rate, so fewer approaches needed)
- ~40 seconds of teacher effort each
- **1** article composition
- 0 podcasts required

…to produce the same distribution event.

### Why the small ask converts

A profile asks someone to be **the subject**. A comment asks them to be **an authority**.
The second is flattering at near-zero exposure — you are not asking them to reveal anything,
you are asking what they think. Warmth is a by-product; the real mechanism is inverted
vulnerability.

### The first contribution is a firewall demo

The contributor sends a voice note, YiM sends back exactly how their words were used, they
approve, it publishes as agreed. At trivial stakes, they have watched the editorial process
work *on them*. The later profile invitation then lands on **evidence**, not rapport — which
is far harder for a competitor to replicate.

**Practical rule:** always send the approval, even when the quote is two lines and obviously
fine. The approval step *is* the pitch. Skipping it because it's trivial wastes the mechanism.

**Practical rule:** ask once, never chase. The non-response only stays costless if it is
genuinely treated as a non-event. A follow-up converts it back into recruitment and spends
the option you were protecting.

## 4. The evidence — two Instagram posts, August 2026

|  | Yin TT journal post | "The real teachers of YiM" reel |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Topic / article promo | 5 teacher portraits, one line each |
| Views | 1,500 | 3,200 |
| Collaborators | 3 | 5 |
| Likes | 16 | 100 |
| Followers gained | 3 | **50** |
| Follow rate | 0.20% | **1.56%** |
| Like rate | 1.07% | 3.12% |
| Views per collaborator | 500 | 640 |

### The finding

- **Reach scaled with collaborators.** Views per collaborator is roughly flat (500 vs 640), so
  each collaborator contributes approximately their own audience. Reach went up 2.1x on 1.67x
  the collaborators — consistent.
- **Conversion did not.** Followers went up **16.7x**; follow rate went up **7.8x**. Nothing
  about collaborator count explains that.

> **Collaborators buy eyeballs. Portraits buy the audience.**

### Why the portrait format converts

A topic post tells someone what one article was about. Five named faces under *"The real
teachers of Yoga in Melbourne"* tells them what the publication **is**, and gives five separate
chances that a viewer thinks *"that's my teacher."* A follow is a bet on future content — only
the portrait reel made the future legible.

### The trap to avoid

The topical-article model ("Does music belong in a yoga class?") is **structurally a Yin TT
post** — it is topic-led. Executed as a topic reel it risks five collaborators' reach at 0.2%
conversion: ~3,000 views and six followers.

**Always ship topical articles as portrait cards.** Five faces, names large, one line of that
teacher's actual view as the caption on their own card. The topic is the frame; the people stay
the subject.

## 6. The constraint that will actually bind

Not teacher supply, and not topic supply.

Topics are abundant and self-sourcing — they arise in class, which Mark attends daily. Better
still, they arrive **attached to a contributor who has already spoken on them**: a teacher makes
a claim in a real class, which becomes the seed quote and contributor #1, already secured. The
other four then face a *reactive* ask ("X said this — what's your take?"), which is smaller
again than an open one.

This also fixes the editorial-thinness risk. Five people answering an editor's survey question
is a listicle. A claim made in a real class with four peers responding is **reported**. Someone
running yoga roundups from a desk structurally cannot do this.

*Capture risk:* good lines evaporate between the mat and the car. A voice note to self in the
car park, before anything else, is the whole system.

### The real constraint: fresh audiences

**A follow is a one-time action per person.** The reel converted at 1.56% because five
audiences were seeing YiM properly for the first time. Re-running the format with the same five
would deliver similar reach at a fraction of the follows — and would look like the format
failing when in fact a spent audience was re-addressed.

So: the portrait format is the engine; **frictionless access to fresh teacher audiences is the
fuel.** This is why deliberately spanning catchments (St Kilda / Brighton / Richmond / Fitzroy /
Brunswick) beats five teachers from one suburb, and why rotation matters.

## 7. The operating pattern

```
topic overheard in class
   ↓
5 teachers — non-overlapping catchments, rotated so audiences stay fresh
   ↓  (3 established + 2 new)
40-second voice notes
   ↓
portrait cards: face, name large, one line of their view
   ↓
collaborative Reel  +  article on site
   ↓
new followers → site traffic → schedules
   ↓
some contributors become future profiles
```

**Note the economy:** one 40-second voice note produces *both* the card caption and the article
quote. The card is the article's raw material, rendered. Sourcing model and winning format are
the same object.

### Topic selection rule

**Real disagreement, zero career cost.** Music in class qualifies — teachers genuinely split,
nobody ever lost work over it. Studio pay rates, booking-platform lock-in and cultural
appropriation all fail: real division, high personal cost. They torch conversion and burn cold
contacts. Save them for contributors who have been through the process twice.

Secondary test: **answerable in 40 seconds with no preparation.** If it needs thinking time,
it's a profile question, not a contribution question.

### Planning heuristic

Reach ≈ **550 × collaborators**. Eight collaborators ≈ 4,500 views. Rough — n=2, and teachers'
audiences differ.

## 8. Open questions

1. **Where does the baseline settle?** Re-check the GA daily trend around 21–24 Aug. Settling
   at 6+/day means one reel permanently doubled the floor and the format compounds. Back to 3
   means attention is rented and cadence is everything.
2. **What is the source of the tail?** If Aug 13–14 visitors still arrive from Instagram, it's
   slow decay of one event. If they arrive via **organic search**, pages are indexing and
   pulling on their own — a materially better story, and the one the monetisation roadmap needs.
3. **Landing pages.** Homepage-only is attention; teacher pages with schedules is intent. If
   arrivals bounce off the front door, point the reel's link somewhere specific.
4. **Does contributor → profile conversion actually happen?** Run three topical articles. If
   zero contributors convert, the small door leads nowhere and the model is a content treadmill
   with no asset accumulation.
5. **Does the collab effect survive weaker incentive?** Profiled teachers had maximum reason to
   amplify — the reel was about them. Voice-note contributors have less. Manufacture the
   personal return: their own card, name prominent, link to their page and schedule.

## 9. What has *not* changed

- Profiles continue. They are the compounding asset and the reason the site is a destination
  rather than a feed.
- Podcasts continue. They were never overhead — they were miscounted as the price of a Reel.
- **The firewall is absolute.** Nothing publishes without the subject's approval; coverage is
  never for sale. The contributor model *increases* approval coordination (five people, not
  one), so expect topical pieces to have a slower path to publish, not a faster one.
- Aggregated cross-studio schedules remain the moat. Topical articles are commoditised by
  nature — any competitor can run one next week. **Therefore: every contributor in every
  topical article links to their profile or to a schedule-bearing contributor stub**, so each
  article builds the asset instead of sitting beside it.

## 10. The strategic logic in one paragraph

A broad, citywide, engaged Instagram following is what makes YiM matter: it transforms
"will you contribute to my website?" into "would you like to be featured in Yoga in
Melbourne?" — the same ask, transformed by the audience behind it. But the following is the
**engine of power, not the store of value**. It lives on Meta's platform and can be repriced by
an algorithm change overnight. The store of value is what the following pumps into: site
traffic, schedule pages, attribution data — the asset YiM owns. The full chain: portrait
collabs → citywide following → publication relevance and recruiting power → more and better
contributors → more content and traffic → the site asset compounds → monetisation. Instagram
is stages one through four. If it ends at stage four — big following, thin site traffic —
YiM has built a media brand Meta owns instead of a directory Mark owns. Keep every reel link
pointed at the site.

## 11. The homepage, demoted and liberated

If reels deep-link to teacher pages, the homepage stops being the front door — which it was
never going to be anyway (side-door traffic dominates all modern publishing; the 61% homepage
share on 10–15 Aug was an artifact of untargeted reel links, not homepage demand).

Four jobs remain, none of which is "landing page": the **identity check** (visited second —
someone likes a teacher page and taps the wordmark to ask "what is this?"); the **B2B pitch**
(teachers deciding whether to contribute, later studios deciding whether to pay, look here
first); the **head term** ("yoga in melbourne" search — compounds for years, needs nothing
weekly); and eventually the **return habit** (where's a class tonight — a utility function).

None of these requires editorial cadence. They require **currency**, and the pull pipeline
provides that free: a homepage anchored on live timetable data cannot go stale, because the
data underneath it moves on its own. Principle: **editorial cadence lives on Instagram and in
the articles; homepage freshness is automated.** Build it as masthead + live directory, not
magazine front page.

Consequence of deep-linking: every teacher page inherits front-door duties in miniature —
clear YiM framing, a route to other teachers, a route to the latest article. The publication
must be legible from any entry point.

## 11c. Operational rule — stories are ONE rolling 24h reel

Instagram has no discrete "stories". Everything posted within 24 hours appears as one continuous
sequence. Posting Monday's frames while Sunday's were still live produced a single 10-frame story.

**The fix is the fixed posting time, which turns out to be functional and not just ritual:** post
at the same hour every evening, ~24h apart, so the previous day's frames expire as the new ones go
up. **Never delete old frames to make room** — deletion destroys their insights permanently.

Supporting discipline: keep Settings → Story → *Save to Archive* on; screenshot Insights at ~20h
before expiry; save each day's set to a Highlight if it's worth keeping visible.

## 12. The content season and the rotation ledger

**A voice-note batch is not one post — it's a season.** Five contributors, one topic, six-plus
assets: the collab reel on day one (acquisition — five fresh audiences), then the five solo
reels spaced ~2/week (retention, relationship currency, deep-link traffic). One sourcing
effort funds ~3 weeks of continuous presence. Batch B's collab opens the next season as
batch A's solos run out. No dead air, no famine.

Solo reel rules: two-way collab with that teacher; link goes to **her page**, tagged
`utm_campaign=solo-<name>`; format varies from the group card (longer quote, schedule mention,
"where to find her this week"); slot assigned by real-world hook (workshop, schedule change)
where one exists.

**Scheduling rule:** order solos by **time-since-last-exposure of the audience**, longest rest
first — NOT by current page traffic. Page views are a lagging echo of Mark's own promotions

## 12a. The format trilogy and the utility layer (added 15 Aug, late PM)

Three Instagram formats, three jobs:

1. **Collab reel** — acquisition. Five fresh audiences per batch. Season opener.
2. **Solo reel** — relationship + deep-link traffic. Spaced ~2/week (Sun + Wed evenings).
3. **Line-up** — utility + return habit. The schedule graph rendered as content. **This is the

- *Daily line-up* = morning **story** on strong days only. Stories are expected daily, carry

  A daily reel would train the audience and algorithm to skip YiM and drag down collab

Verification rule for all line-ups: check against studio timetables before posting (pull data
misses cancellations and covers). Frame as "our teachers", not "Melbourne" — honest at 5,

Link tag: `utm_campaign=sunday-lineup` / `utm_campaign=daily-lineup`.

## 13. The third format: the utility layer (added 15 Aug evening)

Alongside **collab reels** (acquisition) and **solo reels** (relationship + deep links), a third
format emerged and it is the one only YiM can run: **the timetable itself as content.**

Pulled live from `data/schedule.json`, Sunday 16 Aug produced 5 classes / 3 teachers /
3 studios / 3 suburbs, morning to evening — one card, zero sourcing cost. **This is the moat
rendered as content.** No competitor can post it: Mindbody can't see Momence, and every studio
can only show its own timetable. It gets automatically better as the roster grows.

**Cadence rule: always tomorrow, never today.** Nobody plans a 6am class at 6am — the decision
happens the evening before. Evening-before also catches teachers at home with phone in hand, so
their reshare runs overnight and through the morning decision window. And "tomorrow" reads as
an invitation; "today" reads as a report.

**Feed vs story split** (this is what stops the format cannibalising the editorial reels):

| | Daily | Weekly |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Story | Reel |
| Why | Expected daily, 24h life, no grid clutter, no algorithmic price, **carries link stickers** | One utility tentpole per week is a feature; seven would be wallpaper |

Post nothing on thin days. Current roster classes/day: Mon 6, Tue 5, Wed 6, **Thu 1, Fri 2,
Sat 1**, Sun 5 — a one-class card advertises smallness. Silence costs nothing in stories.

**Instagram mechanics established 15 Aug:**

- Collab (dual-author) works on **feed posts and reels only** — max 5 collaborators. **Not on
  stories.**
- Stories: **one link sticker per frame, each frame can point somewhere different.** This is
  how per-teacher deep links get delivered.
- **Reels cannot carry link stickers at all** — reel traffic arrives via bio link/profile.
- Therefore post both: **reel = reach vehicle, story sequence = click vehicle**, same cards
  (1080×1920 works for both).
- Story distribution runs on **@mention → "Add to your story" reshare**. Mention each teacher on
  her own frame only (the frame she reshares is then entirely about her). Mentions unlimited per
  frame; links are not.
- Music: per-frame only, no continuous track. Score the reel, leave the story silent.
- **When a teacher reshares: react warmly, one line, do NOT re-reshare an echo** (only reshare
  if she added her own words). The real value is that replying opens a **DM thread** — which is
  where the next ask travels. The Line-Up quietly builds the DM infrastructure the contributor
  model runs on.

Card generation is automatable: the build script already turns schedule data into pages;
teaching it to emit a daily story card is the same trick. On the working-session list.

## 14. The ladder — and its missing bottom rung

The smallest door is not the request for comment. It is **inclusion in the timetable**:

| Rung | Ask | Teacher effort | Exposure risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. **Listed** | be in the schedule | none | none |
| 1. Contributor | 40-second voice note | minimal | low |
| 2. Solo feature | agree to a reel | none | low |
| 3. Profile | interview | high | high |

Rung 0 **inverts who gives first**: YiM gives (free weekly promotion) before it ever asks. By
the time the voice-note invitation arrives, the teacher has been receiving for weeks.

**Framing — corrected 16 Aug.** An earlier draft of this note advised *not* asking ("arrive
with it already made, a courtesy check on a finished gift"). That was wrong, and Mark caught
it. It blurred two different things:

| | What it is | Approval needed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Aggregated schedule data** | factual, public, already on studio sites — directory content | No, in principle |
| **A YiM teacher page** | photo, standfirst, characterisation — *editorial* | **Yes, always** |

YiM teacher pages are not bare listings; they carry editorial prose. Pre-building one and
presenting it as done would breach the firewall in spirit even where it survives on a
technicality. And more importantly: **the ask is the mechanism, not friction to be optimised
away.** The contact is the entire point of rung 0 — remove it and the rung does nothing. Being
the publication that asks *even when it doesn't have to* is the brand; optimising away the
courtesy would be optimising away the differentiator. The manufactured-no risk is also small —
few teachers decline free promotion.

**The synthesis: show the product, ask the person.** Not an abstract "may we include you?" but
a concrete offer — link a live teacher page as the example, so they can see exactly what
they're being offered, then ask. Concrete demonstration, genuine request.

**Scaling caveat:** the ask scales infinitely, the card does not. 30 teachers = a phone book.
Resolve by separating **inclusion (the site, unlimited — that's the directory)** from
**feature (the card, a rotating weekly selection)**. Better anyway: every yes then grows the
schedule graph, which is the actual moat.

**Real cost per yes is pipeline, not relationship.** A teacher at an un-ingested studio is
manual data forever. Capacity to say yes is bounded by studio feed coverage — which reframes
feed expansion from plumbing into a growth lever.

## 14a. The YiM loop (Mark's formulation, 16 Aug)

```
unknown teacher  →  listed in On the Mat  →  tagged in Stories  →  reshares YiM content
      ↑                                                                      ↓
      └────  collab reels recruit new teacher followers  ←  profiled  ←  familiar relationship
                                                              ↑              ↓
                                                        in a Collab  ←  contributes to an article
```

**It is a loop, not a funnel.** The teachers at the bottom produce the collab reels that
recruit the teachers at the top. Output feeds input — which is why it compounds and why a
funnel diagram understates it.

**Two entry points:** inbound (a teacher who found YiM via a collab reel — warm, created by the
loop itself) and outbound (cold DM — viable only because rung 0 is a one-message ask).

**Most teachers should stop partway, and that is the design working.** A funnel implies everyone
ought to reach the bottom; here the **wide top is the asset** — a large base of listed teachers
*is* the schedule graph, the directory, the moat. Only a handful need to reach a profile.
Don't push people up who are happy where they are.

**Progression signal: the reshare.** Free, observable, and a revealed preference rather than a
stated one. Who reshares tells you who to invite upward — without asking anyone anything.
Track it in the rotation ledger alongside exposure and last contact.

**Two adjacent rungs, deliberately different:** *quoted in an article* is low commitment (a
voice note, a mention). *Formal Instagram Collaborator* is higher — the post lives permanently
on her grid. Treat them as separate steps and offer the second only to teachers who have
already reshared.

**Studios run a parallel loop fed by this one:** a studio with five listed teachers is a warm
sales prospect who is already receiving traffic. The teacher loop is the studio pipeline.

reintroduces the repetition problem. **Localise the asset, not the feed:** `catchment.json`
already defines 8 catchments with full suburb lists, so catchment pages cost nothing, capture
high-intent local search ("yoga in Brighton"), and fragment no audience. The site can be
localised at any density; the feed cannot.

**Threshold: a catchment earns its own card at ~5 teachers.** Until then, citywide reel +
localised site.

**Free-marginal-cost recruitment map:** four studios are already ingested with **no teacher
attached** — Happy Melon (Armadale), (Here) Yoga (Port Melbourne), (Here) Yoga (Malvern),
Kozen (Hawthorn). Recruiting there costs zero pipeline work.

**Tension, flagged honestly:** §12 argues for spreading contributors across non-overlapping
catchments (maximises reach); localisation argues for density (maximises relevance and
sellability). Both right, in sequence — spread serves acquisition, density serves *selling*. A
Bayside with 8 teachers and 5 studios is a proposition for Bayside studio owners; a scattered
roster isn't. It is also the licensing template: YiM won't license "Melbourne", it will license
a repeatable local-density playbook. **Keep recruitment opportunistic citywide, but densify one
catchment first — Bayside, already ahead at 14 classes.**
