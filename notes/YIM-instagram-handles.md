## The collaborator ceiling

Instagram allows **five collaborators on a post or reel including the original
creator** (verified 8 Sep 2026), and the limit is the same for feed posts and
reels. With @yoga_in_melbourne as the creator that leaves **four slots**.

Rayne's post uses all four: @yogawithrayne, @the_space_between_yn,
@warrioroneyoga, @sarahhammondyoga. Nothing can be added without removing
something. Collaborators can be invited after posting, so the set can be changed
later if needed.

**Inndriya is not in that set**, and Rayne teaches two of her six weekly classes
there. Warrior One gets tagged and Inndriya does not. That is a defensible call,
since Warrior One carries three classes plus the teacher training, but it is a
visible choice between two businesses who are both future subscription
customers. Worth making deliberately rather than by running out of slots.

## How to add one

Take it from the person, or from a collaboration that has already gone live where
both accounts are shown. Do not take it from a search result, a tag in someone
else's post, or memory. Record the source and the date in the table.

## Where these get used

* Reel captions, as the collaborator and studio tags
* Collaboration invitations, which outperform plain mentions: the 19 Aug collab
  reel returned 3,200 views and 50 followers from five collaborators, and
  mentioned story frames beat un-mentioned ones 123 to 36, a 3.4x multiple
* Story mentions when a profile or episode goes live
