# What actually produces followers — Instagram, 19 August 2026

## Conclusion

**Follows come from warm, local, 25–44 audiences, and the post format decides
whether you reach them.** Three pieces, measured 19 Aug:

| | 5 teachers (reel) | Alessia (carousel post) | Zoe (reel) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Posted | 9 Aug | 6 Aug | 19 Aug |
| Collaborators | 5 | 1 | 1 |
| Views / viewers | 3,407 / 1,760 | 678 / 322 | 356 / 261 |
| **Follows** | **53** | **8** | **0** |
| Profile visits | 47 | 14 | 0 |
| Bio link taps | 9 | 4 | 0 |
| **Follows per viewer** | **3.01%** | **2.48%** | **0.00%** |
| Profile visits per viewer | 2.67% | 4.35% | 0.00% |
| From Reels tab + Explore | 7.0% | **0%** | **41.5%** |
| Age 13–17 | 0.1% | 0% | 10.5% |
| Age 25–44 | 49.9% | 64.6% | 31.2% |
| Australia | 86.9% | n/c | 56% |
| Followers / non-followers | 13 / 87 | 14.9 / 85.1 | 10.7 / 89.3 |

The two pieces that stayed inside a Melbourne network converted at 2.5–3%. The one
that got algorithmic reach converted at **zero**. More reach, worse result.

## The reasoning, compressed

**Alessia is the control.** One collaborator, 322 viewers — a fifth of the
five-teacher reel and a similar size to Zoe's — and she converted at 2.48%. So the
five-teacher result is not explained by pooling five audiences. Collaborator *count*
is not the variable.

**Cold distribution is the variable.** Alessia's post drew 76.3% from Feed and 21.7%
from Profile and **nothing at all** from Reels tab or Explore. Zoe's reel drew 41.5%
from those two surfaces. That is where the 10.5% teenagers and the 44% non-Australian
viewers came from.

**Format chooses the audience.** A carousel post stays in-network. A reel is pushed
to strangers. This is not a content judgement — it is the distribution surface doing
what it does. Zoe's reel had the most reach of the three per hour and the worst
acquisition.

**Shape over time differs too.** Alessia's post is still adding views and follows 13
days on, a slow warm compounding curve. Zoe's plateaued within two hours.

## What to do with it

1. **For follows, use the carousel post, not the reel.** Same assets, in-network
   distribution. The Zoe podcast set was built with both a reel cover and a feed
   cover — run the feed cover.
2. **Reels are reach, not acquisition.** Fine to make; do not judge them on follows,
   and do not spend production time there while the roster is eight people.
3. **Judge every piece on follows-per-viewer and profile visits**, never on views.
4. **Collaborator geography beats collaborator count.** Zoe's following is global
   (33,000 on Insight Timer). Melbourne studio teachers import Melbourne audiences.
5. **The teacher story frame is the cheapest converting unit YiM has** — it carries a
   link sticker to her own page and inherits her audience. Two teacher frames a night
   beat one.

## Open questions, with the metric to check

- **Does Zoe's reel accumulate any profile visits overnight?** If still 0 on 20 Aug it
  is structural, not slow.
- **Does a Melbourne-audience teacher's profile reel convert?** Test with Steph, Rayne
  or Ryan. Prediction: closer to 2.5% than to 0%.
- **Does the closer's view count ever exceed the opener's?** If it does, reshare
  traffic traverses after all and frame order matters. Watch nightly.
- **Follower growth rate.** 61 follows landed 6–9 Aug. Check the next fortnight's total
  against that to see whether warm supply is thinning.

## Assumptions flagged

- **Measured:** every figure above is from Instagram Insights or GA4 screenshots taken
  by Mark on 18–19 Aug 2026, and from frame-level story insights at ~13h age.
- **Not like-for-like:** the five-teacher reel has had 10 days, Alessia's post 13, Zoe's
  reel 9 hours. All three had plateaued, so the *rates* are comparable even though the
  totals are not final.
- **Small numbers.** Zoe: 261 viewers. 0 versus an expected ~5 follows is partly luck.
  The direction is clear; the precision is not.
- **Inferred, not proven:** that carousel format *causes* in-network distribution. It
  fits all three observations and matches how the surfaces work, but it has not been
  tested by running the same content both ways. That is the experiment worth running.

## Unchanged

The editorial firewall. None of this touches it: aggregated schedule data stays
factual and needs no approval, a profile still requires an interview and her yes, and
coverage is never for sale. Acquisition tactics change which format carries the work,
never what may be published about whom.
