## Rules that came out of it — apply these, don't re-ask

**Teacher Training means trainings that certify.** Momence tags anything
multi-week as `course`, which would have put a $175 four-week study course beside
$1150 and $3000 certifications. Mark moved The Subtle Body to Events. **The feed's
`kind` is a suggestion, not the answer** — a course for practitioners is a
workshop; a course that qualifies someone to teach is training. A 30-minute info
session *for* a 200-hour training belongs in Teacher Training, because the reader
who wants it is the one deciding where to study next.

**A feed title is not a headline.** Momence titles are typed into a booking
system, not written for a page: "The Cyclical Practice - How menstrual cycle
awareness can evolve the way you practice and teach yoga" is 88 characters into a
card built for about 40. Trim to the name, move the rest into the blurb. Same for
"75min Energy Balancing Sound Bath with Phoebe Dubar (IKSRE)" and for anything
carrying its price in the title ("Community Kirtan - By Donation" → title
"Community Kirtan", price "By donation").

**Suburb in the caption, studio underneath.** The composed caption reads
`Sat 22 Aug · South Yarra · $50` with `Within` on the line below — the shape every
hand-written card on the page already had. Naming the studio in both places reads
like a template nobody looked at.

**Two events by the same practitioner are not a duplicate.** IKSRE appears at
Warrior One on 30 Aug and Grass Roots on 11 Sep. Different studios, six weeks
apart, both published. Only drop where the *same event* has two entries — which
happened twice here, and both times because a Momence booking link and a studio
page are different URLs for one thing.

## One decision that sits against a standing rule

Mark chose to publish **"Georgia H"** verbatim from the Good Vibes feed rather
than trim the surname initial. That is his call and it is what shipped. It does
sit against the standing decision that *the person decides how her name is
written, not the feed* — a booking-system initial is not a choice she made. Worth
raising once if she is ever profiled, and not worth raising again before then.
