## Podcast

**Janita's episode is a draft on Transistor on purpose. Do not publish it.**
*Decided before 18 Aug 2026. Recorded 18 Aug after a session flagged it as a fault.*

The audio is served on `janita-doelken.html` from its direct media URL, so anyone on her
profile can hear it. It is deliberately absent from the RSS feed.

**Why:** Apple Podcasts and Spotify order a show newest-first. The feed's top slot is the
one that gets seen, and it should hold whatever YiM is currently promoting. Publishing an
older recording now would take that slot and jumble the running order.

So a YiM episode has two independent states — **playable on the site** and **in the
feed** — and they are set separately. An episode being audible on a teacher's page is not
evidence that it should be in the feed.

**Consequence to accept:** `conversations.html` lists fewer episodes than the site plays.
That is correct, not stale.

**Open option, Mark's call:** Transistor supports publishing with a back-dated release
date. Janita's episode could enter the feed *below* the newest one rather than above it —
her episode reaches Apple and Spotify subscribers, the top slot is undisturbed, and
existing subscribers are not pushed a notification for it. The cost is that some apps
surface back-dated episodes poorly. Not done; noted as available.

## Instagram

**The closer frame stays, even though it is always the weakest.**
*Decided 18 Aug 2026.*

Measured three times: 30 views on 15 Aug, 30 on 17 Aug, 16 on 18 Aug — the lowest frame
every time. It is kept anyway. At a small roster a four-frame story rolls past before
anyone settles into it, and the closer buys length. **Volume is the job, not views.**

Retire it on **roster size, not performance** — about six teachers on a normal day. Move
the "full timetable" call to action onto the line-up frame first; the closer is currently
the only frame carrying it.

**On the Mat's product is a teacher who said yes, not a visitor.**
*Decided 17 Aug 2026, correcting the session's assumption.*

The daily story is a recruitment instrument. The **collab reel** is the acquisition
engine — 3,200 views and 50 followers from five collaborators, against a story that was
reshared by every mentioned teacher and returned 7 link clicks. Do not judge On the Mat by
its own view counts.

## Teacher data

**The person decides how her name is spelled, not the feed.**
*Two for two, 17–18 Aug 2026.*

Steph is **Philip**, one L — (Here) Yoga's "Phillip" is the alias. Rach is **Rach** —
(Here) Yoga's "Rachael" is the alias. In both cases the feed looked canonical and was
wrong, and cross-feed reconciliation could not have caught it. Ask at onboarding, before
a URL is minted and crawled.

**A studio's timetable cannot be inferred from another studio's.**
*Decided 18 Aug 2026.*

Brighton and Mordialloc are different catchments with independent schedules. An earlier
attempt to infer Mordialloc's slot grid from Brighton's called a correct row suspicious.

**Observed class counts include substitutions and mislead in both directions.**
*Decided 18 Aug 2026.*

Franks Martin reads as Brighton's busiest teacher on ten classes a week; most is covering.
Shelley Armstrong reads as a two-class teacher and teaches about five, because four of hers
sit under a covering teacher in the window. Do not rank recruitment targets on class count.

**A dark feed means the data is unverifiable, not stale.**
*Decided 18 Aug 2026.*

The repo stores the last successful *pull* date. That is not the same as when a human last
checked the times. Warrior One's rows were correct for three weeks after its feed went dark.
Studios now carry a `verified` date — use it, and do not tell Mark data is stale on pull
date alone.

## Editorial

**The firewall is absolute and is not a trade-off to be balanced.**

Aggregated schedule data is factual and public — no approval needed. A teacher **page** is
editorial and never publishes without her yes. Coverage is never for sale. A listing (rung 0)
carries no editorial content at all: no essence line, no pull quote, no portrait, no player.
If a sentence about who she is wants writing, it belongs in a profile, and the profile needs
her approval first.
