Yoga in Melbourne

So what is this? A directory? A journal? A podcast?

In some ways it is all of these things. But step back a little, and what this is comes into view.

Yoga in Melbourne is a map of the fabric that holds our community together. Over the coming years it will come to life here, story by story, through the people who have made yoga their life's work.

We look forward to sharing with you the richness and depth of our encounters with Melbourne's teachers and studios. These are stories of transformation, of dedication and of joy.

So please, enjoy the thing that this is.
